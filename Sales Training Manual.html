1-Hour Session — Terms & Conditions
After the agent reads the T&C independently, hold a 1-hour meeting to cover the key points.
ℹ️

Before the meeting: Ask the agent to read the Terms & Conditions on their own first. This session is to clarify and highlight the most important points — not to read it together.

1

This is an Online Service — No Physical Contract (Clause 2)

There is no paper contract to sign. According to clause 2 of the T&C, the contract becomes legally binding in one of two ways:

  • The customer downloads and logs in to the app or customer portal.
  • The customer activates call forwarding to our service and receives at least one call.
💳

Always make clear that a payment method must be provided before the service starts — this confirms the customer is signing up for a paid service.

2

Cancellation & Notice Periods (Clause 9-1)

According to clause 9-1, there is no minimum contract period. There is only a notice period, which depends on how long the customer has been with us:

Customer TenureNotice Period
First 2 months (including trial month)❌ No notice — ends same day
Month 3 → End of Year 1📅 1 month's notice
Year 2📅 2 months' notice
Year 3 and beyond📅 3 months' notice (maximum)
💡

Why longer notice for older customers? Our system predicts staffing based on long-term customers' call volume. A longer notice period gives us time to adjust our staffing needs without disruption.

3

How the Customer Must Cancel (Clause 9-4)

According to clause 9-4, it is the customer who must cancel the service, notifying us by phone, email, or through the app or customer portal.

4

When the Company Can Cancel the Contract (Clause 9-2)

According to clause 9-2, in rare cases we can end a contract without the customer's agreement. The only valid reasons are:

  • The account is identified as fraud.
  • We are legally unable to handle certain calls (e.g., life-critical or emergency calls).
  • We don't have the staff capacity to handle the required call volume.

Sugar CRM — Introduction & Practice
Covers courses: Sugar Introduction + Account Overview

What is Sugar CRM?

Sugar CRM is our central database where everything is recorded: calls, accounts, emails, tasks, cases, and more. Other tools (like the dialer) connect and sync data into Sugar, but Sugar itself does not modify those tools — it is the single source of truth. When in doubt, always check Sugar.

🍬 Open Sugar CRM

💡 Log in with your regular username without the @ and your usual password.

🖱️

Navigation tip: Click the ☰ (3 lines) at the top to expand the left menu. Then click the ⋯ (3 dots) — located to the right of the Home button — to access and switch between dashboards, including the Sales Dashboard shown below.

📊 Walk the agent through the Sales Dashboard — it shows:

📞

Planned Calls for Today

Calls the agent has scheduled themselves, with time and description.

📈

Today's Statistics

A live view of the agent's performance metrics for the current day.

✅

Pending Tasks

Tasks the agent needs to complete. These are linked to cases and help track progress toward personal goals.

Sugar Left Menu — Explained
Walk the agent through each section of the blue sidebar, one by one.
🏢
Accounts

All validated accounts — whether they are potential customers, current customers, or past customers. This is the main list of companies we work with or are working toward.

📞
Calls

Every call that has been made and logged in Sugar. The majority are from Sales agents, but you will also see some customer service calls here.

🗂️
Cases

Whenever a customer makes a request or something requires action, a case is created. Customer service handles most cases, but Sales also creates cases — for example, when a sale is closed, the seller opens a case so customer service can call the new client to configure their profile in detail.

⭐
Feedback

Customers can rate their calls on a scale of 1 to 5. Here is how the system handles each rating:

⭐⭐⭐
Rating 1, 2 or 3 → Complaint
A case is automatically created for customer service to investigate and resolve the issue.
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Rating 4 or 5 → Feedback
No case is created. Customer service can still read any comments left by the customer.
✅
Tasks

Tasks always live inside a case and represent what was done to resolve it. For example, a case called "Requests tariff change" might have a task called "Change Tariff", with the old and new tariff written in the description.

💼
Opportunities

These are selling points. When an opportunity is closed (won), the agent earns points. Points accumulate throughout the month and translate into a monthly bonus added to salary. The sales team will explain this in detail later.

✉️
Emails

All emails sent and received are logged in Sugar. Customer service manages the highest volume, but Sales also sends emails regularly from within the system.

🎯
Leads

Leads are potential new customers. They can enter the system in three ways: created manually by an agent, created automatically when someone contacts us through an official channel, or imported from a purchased list from a third-party provider.

Official channels that auto-create leads:

  • 📞 Calling our Sales phone number
  • ✉️ Emailing our Sales inbox
  • 🌐 Completing the website contact form
  • 🖥️ Starting a Live Test from our website
✅ Web form or Live Test leads
The prospect left their details voluntarily — minimal verification needed, ready to work on immediately.
⚠️ Phone or email leads
These require validation — the contact may be a mistake, an existing customer, or may not meet our criteria.

Sugar Search Bar
How to find any record quickly and accurately.
1

Be as precise as possible

You can search by any data, but the more specific you are, the better the results. Searching by a first name might return hundreds of matches — searching by a customer number (RAV) or email address will return exactly the right record.

2

Filter by category to speed things up

You can limit the search to a specific record type to get faster, cleaner results. For example: if you are looking for a lead, select the Lead category, type your search term, and press Enter. The same applies for Accounts, Cases, and so on.

Account Overview — Page Layout
Two ways to navigate: horizontal tabs across the top, and vertical collapsible menus down the page.
📐

There are two navigation systems on an account page: horizontal tabs (left side, across the top) and vertical menus (collapsed panels down the page). We'll cover both.

📂 Vertical Menus — Recommended Order for Sales Agents

💡

Start by closing all vertical menus, then show the agent how to reorder them to match this recommended layout for Sales.

📞
Calls

All calls recorded here are from Sales agents only — they are the only ones who log calls directly to the main account. Each call entry includes the date, time, and relevant notes or outcome from the Sales agent.

✉️
Emails

All emails sent to and received from this customer are automatically sorted here. For prospects (not yet customers), these are managed by Sales. Once they become active customers, all email communication is handled by Customer Service.

👤
Contacts

All phone numbers, email addresses, and contacts linked to this account. You can call directly by clicking the phone icon next to a number — but make sure CAI is open and unlocked before doing so.

💼
Opportunities

Each opportunity is a bonus-earning selling point. On the right side you can see how many points each opportunity is worth. As a rule, selling a service earns more points than adding a payment method or creating a sub-account. Customer Service agents can also earn bonuses through opportunities — for example, by agreeing to add a 24-hour service or a sub-account with an existing customer.

🗂️
Cases

Any time something needs to be done for a customer, a case is created. Every case contains a task that must be closed with a description of what was done — so everything is properly recorded in the system.

🎯
Leads

The original lead that was linked to this account before the prospect became a customer.

📁
Documents

Any legal documents the customer has sent us that are required to maintain the contract — for example, proof of business existence or a change of ownership document if the business was sold.

🚫

The remaining vertical menus are not necessary to review at this stage. Move on to the horizontal tabs below.

📑 Horizontal Tabs — Explained

🪪
Business Card

Basic information about the customer, including their RAV (customer number). Also shows the account category (so you can see whether they are a current customer or not) and the Key Operations Manager — the Customer Service person assigned to manage this account, if applicable.

📋
Contact Details

Information that is automatically synced from the Customer Management Tool when the phone service is set up for this customer.

🏛️
Company Details

Basic company information including the tax number, the date the customer started with us, and the contract duration in days.

📊
Usage Behaviour

Key customer activity data at a glance:

  • Total calls received since the start of the contract
  • Calls in the last 30 days
  • Total number of feedbacks and their average score (1–5)
  • Date the app was first installed
  • Health Score — automatically triggers a case for Customer Service if it drops below a threshold. For example: if a customer normally receives 50 calls/month but only had 5 in the last 30 days, the system creates a case to investigate whether there's a call forwarding issue or any other problem.
💳
Tariff Details

Shows the customer's current tariff plan and any extensions applied to their account. This tab is more relevant for Customer Service and more advanced use cases.

🛠️
Internal Tools

Shortcuts to launch key applications directly from this account: CAI, Customer Management Tool, Customer Portal, and the option to create a new customer. This is also where the Setup Wizard is accessed — the tool used to onboard a new customer after a sale is made, which we will cover in the next practice session.

🧾
Invoice Details

A complete list of all invoices for this customer. Open one and walk the agent through it.

Key elements to highlight in an invoice:

  • Customer number
  • Invoice number
  • Two dates: the lower date is when the invoice was generated; the upper date is when the charge will be applied to the customer's bank account.
  • Tariff section: covers everything included in the fixed monthly fee — the plan itself, voicemail, 24-hour service, sub-accounts, or any other add-ons with a fixed cost. This is charged in advance (like Netflix or a gym membership — you pay before you use it).
  • Service costs section: covers usage-based charges — calls received, minutes used, etc. This is charged in arrears — the customer pays for the previous month's usage.
  • Total amount (including both sections, plus applicable taxes/VAT).
🏦

Good to know: The bank account number is also shown at the bottom of one of the pages, in case the customer prefers to make the payment by manual bank transfer.

Lead Validation Practice
Hands-on exercise: the agent creates a lead and a customer in Sugar, following your guidance step by step.
📞

Trainer preparation (before the session): Ask an AI tool (e.g. ChatGPT) to generate a valid mobile phone number, street address, and postal code for the country you will be training on. Have these ready before starting — you will need them during the exercise. The agent does not need to prepare anything.

1

Start the exercise

Ask the agent to share their screen and open Sugar. They will create a new lead and customer following your instructions.

2

Create a new lead

In the left sidebar, go to Leads. Once inside, click Create in the top-right corner.

3

See which fields are required

On this screen, without typing anything, click Save — this will highlight in red which fields are mandatory.

4

Fill in the lead fields

Now fill in the following fields:

  • First Name: the agent's own first name
  • Last Name: the agent's own last name
  • Account Name: Test-Account + today's date. Example: Test-Account 15.06.2027
  • Email: use this format — agent's first name @ test-date.com. Example: mark@test-15062027.com
  • Campaign: Outbound Upselling
  • Brand: the brand corresponding to the agent's own country
  • Phone Number: enter the phone number prepared earlier
  • Primary Address: enter the street address
  • Change the country to match the agent's country

Then click Save.

5

Open and validate the lead

Open the lead — it will appear first in the list — by clicking on its name. Then click the dropdown arrow next to Edit, and select Validate Lead.

6

Convert the lead into an account

The Validate Lead window opens. If all the previous data was entered correctly, you can continue without filling in anything else: click Interested in our product/service, then Convert without calling. Afterwards, click the red "Copy account URL to clipboard" button and paste it into a new Chrome tab.

7

Launch the Setup Wizard

Inside the account in Sugar, go to Internal Tools and click Create/Add Account. This will open the Setup Wizard.

8

Complete the required fields

Here you need to complete all fields marked with a red asterisk *.

9

Intracommunity VAT field

🇪🇸🇮🇹

For Spain and Italy agents: Intracommunity VAT (VIES) is an EU registry that allows businesses in different EU countries to trade with each other without charging VAT on the invoice, as long as both parties are registered in the system. If our customer is registered for intracommunity VAT, they won't pay VAT with us.

If this wasn't asked during the sale, it's simple: just add IT for Italy or ES for Spain in front of the company's tax ID. Example: ES49059147B. If it shows an error, it means the company is not registered for intracommunity VAT — in that case, leave this field empty and enter the NIF/CIF without the country prefix in the field below.

10

Move to the next step

Go to the bottom-right corner and click to advance to the next step. If there's an error, go to the corresponding field and fill it in or correct it.

11

Set up the phone service

If you're on the Customer Details screen, scroll down to Available Services, then click Contract a phone service.

  • Password: 123456
  • Language: the agent's language
  • Mobile: click and select the one shown as recommended
  • Click Next
12

Greeting, forwarding & promotional package

  • Click Greeting and select any option.
  • Standard message: "In a meeting"
  • Forwarding numbers: click and select a phone number, then click Select and mark it as Private.
  • Scroll down to Promotional Package and select 1M 50€. (In a real sale, this depends on the campaign the sales agent is running and what was offered to the new customer.)
  • Start date: set it to tomorrow, so the account can be cancelled afterwards without issues.
💡

Extensions like the 24-hour service could be added here, but for this exercise leave them unmarked. Click Next.

13

Payment method

Now it asks for a payment method — in a real sale you would enter it here. If it's SEPA, it's done on this page; if it's a credit card, click "I don't have a SEPA-eligible bank account" and enter the card there. To continue in this exercise, click "I don't have bank details" — this takes you to the recap screen. Scroll to the bottom and click Next.

14

Finish the setup

Click "Send welcome message with password" and click OK. The account is now created.

15

Good to know: editing customer info later

If you ever need to modify the customer's information after setup, go to Manage Services and scroll down to "Review and change customer data." Open and close it so the agent sees where it is.

16

Good to know: changing the payment method

To change or add a payment method, go to the Manage Services tab and scroll down to "Change payment method." Open and close it so the agent sees where it is.

17

Delete the test account

To delete the account, go to Contract Details, scroll down, and click the red "Cancel the customer (terminate all contracts)" button. At the top, select "We", then mark the "Test customer" option. Leave the date as is and click OK.

🎉

Wrap up: Ask the agent if they have any remaining questions. This concludes the practice exercise — and the training. Wish the agent good luck before ending the session.
